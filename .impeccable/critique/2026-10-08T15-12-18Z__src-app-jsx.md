## Priority Issues

- [P1] Hero answers "how much did we spend", not "how are we doing", and misrepresents it during the ~3s tower animation; same teal tone for a 73% month and a 24% month; over-budget never surfaced in the hero. Fix: lead with 存下 $X（收入的 N%）, demote spending; start from final state / animate only the delta (<1s); add one evaluative line (比上月多存 $X); surface 「N 個預算超支」 in hero. Commands: /impeccable clarify, /impeccable animate.
- [P1] Month bars unreadable and untappable: no month labels, 23px-wide bars as short as 18px, 累計 pill 47×30, dashed future bars unexplained, mode switch silently changes what the big number means. Fix: month labels, ≥44px column hit areas, current-month marker, labeled 月份/累計 segmented control. Commands: /impeccable adapt, /impeccable clarify.
- [P1] Page order buries daily work: hero → 4 charts → budgets → 16 recurring → day list (~1,740px down on mobile); no in-context confirmation after saving. Fix: hero → at-risk budgets → today's list → rest; collapse charts into one 「看分析」 entry; flash/scroll to new row after save. Commands: /impeccable layout, /impeccable distill.
- [P2] Tower meaning depends on tint alone and is inaccessible: no legend, SVG aria-hidden, clickable <g> not focusable, 9.5px income label; bounce easing on the pit. Fix: inline legend with amounts (also keyboard path), 12px ink label, non-overshoot easing. Commands: /impeccable harden, /impeccable typeset.
- [P2] Contrast and shared-login gaps: subtle text 2.3:1 on hero label/chart summaries; tag text 4.3:1; selected tab barely visible; quick sheet has no payer field though payer charts depend on it. Fix: raise labels to muted or darker, darken muted slightly, filled selected tab, payer chip row remembered per device. Commands: /impeccable harden, /impeccable colorize.
